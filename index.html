02 — About

Emotion meets futurism.

I’m Tanmay Sakhare, a graphic designer and video editor from Nagpur, Maharashtra, creating visuals where emotion meets futurism.

With 3 years of editing and 1 year of design experience, my work blends fast execution, unique color grading, strong composition, and creative transitions, shaped by films, sci-fi, music, and neon aesthetics.

I turn ideas into cinematic motion through basic motion graphics, social media design, cinematic edits, and short-form content using Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Canva and CapCut.
